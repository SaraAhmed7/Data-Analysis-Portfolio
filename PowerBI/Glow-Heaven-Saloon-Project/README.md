# 💄 Glow Haven Salon Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

This project was delivered as part of a freelance engagement for a beauty salon business. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of service performance, revenue trends, and marketing effectiveness across different customer segments.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Track overall revenue and business performance
* Analyze customer behavior (new vs returning)
* Evaluate marketing channel effectiveness
* Identify top-performing service types
* Optimize ad spend and conversions

## 📊 Key Insights

* Total Revenue: **315K**
* Total Conversions: **4K**
* Total Ad Spend: **50K**
* Basic and Luxury services generate the highest revenue
* Returning customers contribute significantly to total revenue
* Referral and Walk-in channels show strong performance

  ![Glow-Heaven-Saloon-Project](images/Dashboard.png)

## 📈 Dashboard Features

* Revenue analysis by service type (Basic, Luxury, Premium)
* Conversion analysis by marketing channel (Google Ads, Instagram, Referral, Walk-in, Website)
* Customer segmentation (New vs Returning)
* Revenue trends by time of day and quarter
* Monthly revenue tracking
* Ad spend vs revenue comparison
