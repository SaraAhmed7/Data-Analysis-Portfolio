# 🧁 Sweet Rise Bakery Analysis

## 📌 Overview

This project was developed as part of a freelance engagement for a bakery business. The goal was to design an interactive dashboard that provides clear insights into sales performance, customer behavior, and marketing effectiveness.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Track overall revenue and business growth
* Identify top-performing products and categories
* Analyze customer purchasing behavior
* Evaluate marketing channel performance
* Support data-driven decision-making

## 📊 Key Results

* Total Revenue: **505.84K**
* Average Order Size: **206.85K**
* Total Conversions: **12K**
* Identified peak sales periods across months and times of day
* Highlighted high-performing product categories and channels

## 📈 Dashboard Features

* Revenue trends by month and quarter
* Sales breakdown by product category
* Marketing channel performance analysis
* Order distribution by time of day
* Conversion tracking
* Delivery vs. pickup insights

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Power BI

## 💼 My Role

* Cleaned and prepared raw data
* Designed and developed the full dashboard
* Created interactive visuals for better insights
* Delivered actionable recommendations to the client


## 🚀 Outcome

The dashboard helped the client better understand their business performance and supported more informed decisions regarding sales strategy and marketing efforts.
